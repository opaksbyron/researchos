# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ResearchOS serves researchers and research administrators who need to turn scattered source material into defensible findings. The public showcase also serves technical reviewers and hiring teams who need to understand the product, the implementation boundary, and the evidence that it works without first creating an account.

## Product Purpose

ResearchOS connects an ibl.ai research agent with a source library, durable memory, and project-scoped work. Success means a researcher can add evidence, ask a question, inspect a source-grounded answer, and preserve useful context. The public surface must make that workflow and the engineering behind it understandable in under two minutes.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, calm, and transparent. ResearchOS should feel like a careful research instrument: confident about what it knows, explicit about uncertainty, and precise about what the application author built.

## Anti-references

- Generic chatbot landing pages that lead with spectacle but never show evidence.
- Repeated grids of interchangeable SaaS cards.
- Decorative AI gradients, glass panels, and fake citations.
- Editorial or luxury styling that makes the research workflow feel ornamental.
- Claims of reliability without visible tests, architecture, or reproducible steps.

## Design Principles

- Show the evidence before making the claim.
- Let visitors understand the workflow before asking them to sign in.
- Separate platform capabilities from the application architecture built around them.
- Make readiness, uncertainty, and failure states visible.
- Use one strong research narrative instead of a catalogue of disconnected features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every public interaction must be keyboard reachable, retain visible focus, work without color as the only signal, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Layouts must remain usable at 320px and at 200% zoom.
